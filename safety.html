At Mystical Wellness, transparency and safety are our foundation. Because every Nourishment is prepared fresh with real protein yogurt, raw cut fruit, and wholesome plant milks without chemical preservatives, we uphold strict refrigeration, hygiene, and allergen handling standards.

Storage & Freshness Guidelines

Keep Cold (≤ 38°F)

Refrigerate immediately upon pickup or delivery. Keep stored at or below 38°F (3°C) at all times.

Best Within 4–5 Days

Freshly cut raw whole fruit contains no artificial preservatives. For optimal flavor and texture, consume within 4 to 5 days.

Natural Separation

Natural separation of oat milk, coconut milk, and chia gel may occur over time. Simply give your jar a gentle stir before enjoying.

Allergen Declarations

CONTAINS: Milk (Real Chobani protein yogurt) and Coconut (Coconut milk base & optional coconut flakes).

OPTIONAL ALLERGENS: Sliced almonds are an optional add-on and contain Tree Nuts (Almonds). Almonds are stored in dedicated sealed containers and handled with clean utensils.

GLUTEN & OATS: Our recipe is made with pure oat milk and chia seeds. While oats do not naturally contain gluten, they may be processed in facilities that handle grains.

DAIRY NOTICE: This recipe contains real dairy yogurt and is not a dairy-free or vegan product.

FDA Compliance & Transparency

FDA Disclaimer: The statements, nutritional values, and health information shared on this website have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Mystical Wellness Nourishments and products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Nutritional Estimates: Macronutrient figures (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, and fiber) are calculated using standard manufacturer data for the base recipe (protein yogurt, oat milk, coconut milk, chia seeds). Micronutrient values (Sugar, Vitamin C, Potassium, Calcium, Sodium, Iron) are calculated from USDA FoodData Central reference values for the exact raw fruit portions used per serving. These figures are calculated estimates, not laboratory-tested Nutrition Facts labels.

Preparation Standards: Every order is hand-crafted in accordance with local health department food handling practices, using sanitized prep surfaces, temperature-controlled refrigeration, and fresh fruit inspected prior to cutting.
