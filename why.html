Our story

Why we,
make this.

Mystical Wellness started with a simple idea: real food, made in small batches, by someone who actually cares what's in it. No preservatives, no mystery ingredients — just oat milk, coconut milk, chia seeds, and real fruit, prepared fresh every week in El Cajon.

Small batch, on purpose

We don't mass-produce. Every Nourishment is made weekly in limited quantities, which means what you're eating was prepared days — not months — before it reaches you. Freshness isn't a marketing word here; it's the whole point.

Naturally dairy-free

Every base uses oat milk and coconut milk instead of dairy — not as a trend, but because it makes the pudding creamy, naturally sweet, and easy on more stomachs, without sacrificing flavor.

Built around chia

Chia seeds are the foundation of everything we make: fiber, plant-based protein, and a satisfying texture that holds up to real fruit and optional toppings like almonds, coconut flakes, or maple syrup. Curious about the exact numbers? See full nutrition details.

Rooted in El Cajon

We're a local, small business — not a chain, not a franchise. Every order is made by hand and picked up or delivered right here in the community we're part of.
