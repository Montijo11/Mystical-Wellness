Our story

Why we
make this.

Mystical Wellness started with a simple idea: real food, made fresh for you, by someone who actually cares what's in it. No preservatives, no mystery ingredients — just real protein yogurt, oat milk, chia seeds, and real fruit, prepared fresh for every single order.

Fresh every order, on purpose

We don't premake a big batch and let it sit. Every Nourishment is prepared fresh for your specific order, which means what you're eating was made for you — not sitting on a shelf waiting to be picked up. Freshness isn't a marketing word here; it's the whole point.

Protein-powered, honestly

Every base includes real Chobani protein yogurt alongside oat milk and chia seeds — giving each Nourishment roughly 8 grams of protein without any protein powders or additives. This does mean our recipe contains dairy; we'd rather be upfront about that than make a claim that isn't true.

Built around chia

Chia seeds are the foundation of everything we make: fiber, plant-based nutrients, and a satisfying texture that holds up to real fruit and optional toppings like almonds, coconut flakes, or maple syrup. Curious about the exact numbers? See full nutrition details.

Real raw fruit, every time

No syrupy fillers, concentrates, or artificial flavoring — just real mango, strawberry, blueberry, apple, and pineapple folded in fresh. Raw, freshly cut fruit preserves the natural Vitamin C, potassium, and active nutrients that get lost in commercial processing.

Locally owned and operated

We're a local, small business — not a chain, not a franchise. Every order is made by hand and picked up or delivered right here in the community we're part of.
