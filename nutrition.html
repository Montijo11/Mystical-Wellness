Ingredient transparency

Nutrition,
clearly shared.

Every base combines real Chobani plain protein yogurt, Chobani Extra Creamy oat milk, a touch of unsweetened coconut milk, and chia seeds — giving each Nourishment roughly 8 grams of protein before any fruit or toppings. This recipe contains dairy and is not dairy-free.

What's in every base

Chobani Protein plain yogurt — 1/4 cup (contains dairy)
Chobani Oatmilk Extra Creamy — 1/4 cup
Unsweetened coconut milk — 2 teaspoons
Chia seeds — 2 teaspoons
Mango — 1/8 cup
Pineapple — 1 tablespoon
All other fruit (strawberry, blueberry, apple) — 1 tablespoon
Maple syrup — 1 teaspoon, if added
